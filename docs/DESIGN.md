## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the intimacy, precision, and indulgence of high-end editorial beauty publishing combined with digital diagnostic sophistication. Crafted specifically for personal color analysis and curated aesthetic consultations, it transforms technical undertone classification into an elevated self-discovery ritual.

The visual style blends **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Translucency**. Generous negative space, delicate golden hair-lines, and luminous porcelain surfaces mirror the tactile luxury of vellum stationery and gold-embossed cosmetic packaging. The emotional resonance is calm, empowering, and exquisitely polished—eschewing clinical diagnostic austerity in favor of private haute couture salon intimacy.

## Colors

The palette balances warm metallic radiance against soft cosmetic undertones:

- **Primary (`#C5A059`)**: Muted champagne gold serving as the prestige accent. Used for interactive highlights, key actions, framing micro-borders, and active season indicators.
- **Secondary (`#F5E6E8`)**: Rose petal tint evoking skin flush and subtle softness. Applied to secondary surface backgrounds, pill badges, and seasonal warmth accents.
- **Tertiary (`#1F1B18`)**: Deep espresso charcoal. Provides high-contrast editorial legibility without the harshness of pure black, grounding headers and body copy.
- **Neutral (`#FDFBF7`)**: Porcelain cream base canvas. Replaces sterile stark white with warm, reflective luminosity.

### Functional Roles
- **Canvas Base**: Porcelain cream (`#FDFBF7`).
- **Surface Elevation 1**: Pure translucent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`).
- **Hairline Dividers**: Warm metallic champagne at reduced opacity (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.22)`).
- **Muted Editorial Text**: Deep espresso neutral at 65% opacity (`#59534E`).

## Typography

The typography couples high-contrast editorial romance with razor-sharp modern legibility. 

- **Display & Headings (`Playfair Display`)**: Expresses classical elegance, delicate hairline serifs, and high-fashion authority. Used for diagnostic reveals, seasonal title classifications, and editorial pull quotes. Italicized accents within headlines should be used sparingly to highlight key concepts (e.g., *Sắc Tố Ấm* or *Hạ Tinh Tế*).
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Balances the ornate nature of the serif headline with clean, geometric clarity. Its generous x-height and rounded terminals maintain optimal readability for complex undertone guidelines, makeup formulas, and consultation notes in Vietnamese diacritics.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Rendered with subtle tracking (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) in uppercase to mimic luxury catalog indexing and shade numbering.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured **12-column fluid grid** for desktop (`margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`) transitioning to a **4-column grid** on mobile devices (`margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`). 

The spacing strategy is intentionally spacious to allow product imagery and delicate color swatches room to breathe. Editorial pacing relies on alternating between wide panoramic showcase bands and asymmetrical 2-column or 3-column analysis blocks. Section-level vertical rhythm follows multiples of `space-xl` (40px–80px) to establish a calm, unhurried browsing experience.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold drop-shadows, utilizing **warm ambient diffusion** combined with **frosted glassmorphism**:

1. **Surface Base (Level 0)**: Solid porcelain cream (`#FDFBF7`) without shadows.
2. **Card Surfaces (Level 1)**: Translucent milky white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) supported by a 1px border of `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)` and an ambient, warm diffused shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(31, 27, 24, 0.04)`.
3. **Floating Overlays & Draping Panels (Level 2)**: Frosted glass layers utilizing `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with `rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85)` surface fills and gold-tinted glow shadows: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.15)`.
4. **Active Selections & Modals (Level 3)**: Raised cards rimmed with crisp metallic gold (`#C5A059`) and deep warm drop: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(31, 27, 24, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Roundedness Level 2** (base radius of `0.5rem` / 8px). 

- Standard interactive controls (inputs, secondary buttons, diagnostic cards) feature `0.5rem` corner radiuses, striking a balance between classical architectural geometry and soft organic beauty.
- Container panels and imagery holders use `rounded-lg` (`1rem`) to soften photography edges.
- Seasonal status tags, micro-badges, and primary pill actions use full oval curves (`9999px`) to echo the curvature of cosmetic compacts and perfume flacons.
- Color swatches, hue drape samples, and undertone selectors are strictly circular (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Champagne gold fill (`#C5A059`) with espresso text (`#1F1B18`) or pure white, shaped with full pill radius (`9999px`). Subtle inner glow on hover (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary / Editorial Outline Action**: Transparent base framed by a 1px metallic gold border (`#C5A059`), deep charcoal label, transitioning to a soft rose tint (`#F5E6E8`) on hover.

### Seasonal Badges (Hệ Mùa Cá Nhân)
- Pill-shaped labels categorizing the 4 key palettes:
  - *Xuân Ấm Áp (Spring Warm)*: Soft golden apricot tint with subtle shimmer border.
  - *Hạ Tinh Tế (Summer Cool)*: Delicate powder rose (`#F5E6E8`) with soft lavender undertones.
  - *Thu Trầm Ấm (Autumn Warm)*: Deep terra-cotta gold with espresso typography.
  - *Đông Sang Trọng (Winter Cool)*: Crisp porcelain silver with deep espresso contrast.

### Color Swatch Rings & Shimmer Drops
- Circular selectors with a 2px inner white gap and an outer accent ring. Selected states feature a delicate 1.5px gold halo with an animated soft radiant glow.

### Interactive Draping Slider (Thanh Thử Sắc Tố)
- Dual split-view surface with an ultra-thin champagne gold divider handle. The handle contains an embossed central jewel icon, sliding seamlessly across porcelain cream backdrops to preview cool versus warm fabric undertones against user photography.

### Beauty & Cosmetic Cards
- High-key imagery containers featuring `rounded-lg` (16px) corners, frosted bottom information caps, subtle gold hairline dividing rules, and right-aligned metallic price points or shade identifiers (e.g., `#04 Velvet Petal`).

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Nude porcelain fields with a 1px border in `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`. On focus, the field transitions to a solid `#C5A059` border with a subtle warm aura (`0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.12)`). Typography sits in crisp espresso charcoal.
